Legal & title verification for your Mumbai flat

A clear title is the one thing you cannot fix after possession. We trace the ownership chain, pull the encumbrance record and read the agreement, so a dispute surfaces before you sign — not years after.

Check my property → Ask on WhatsApp

Most buyers see one deed and a broker’s assurance. A sound title needs the whole chain: every owner before the seller, every charge ever registered against the flat, and any case running in a court or tribunal over the land it sits on. All of it is on the public record, and almost nobody assembles it.

What we verify

  • Title chain, 30+ years. Every conveyance from the current owner backwards, so no broken link hides a defective transfer.
  • Encumbrance certificate. Registered mortgages, liens and prior sales over the longest period the sub-registrar will give us.
  • Conveyance & society status. Whether the building’s land was actually conveyed to the society — a common Mumbai gap that affects redevelopment and resale.
  • Court & tribunal search. Litigation on the project or the land, including consumer-forum matters filed by other buyers.
  • Land use & FSI. Sanctioned use and floor-space position, so the flat you are buying is one that was permitted to be built.
  • Agreement red-flag review. Possession date and penalty, termination asymmetry, escalation and area-variation clauses read line by line.

How it works

Three steps, no site visit needed from you. Share the property, we pull the records, you get a written result in 48–72 hours.

  1. Send the brief. Property address, project or builder and any documents you already have — over the form or WhatsApp.
  2. We run the checks. An analyst pulls and cross-checks the public records listed above.
  3. You get the result. A written finding with each point sourced, as part of your 0–100 Property Health Score and a clear buy / negotiate / walk-away recommendation.

Questions buyers ask

Do you replace my lawyer?

No. We recommend an advocate reviews the title opinion and agreement before registration. Our verification is wider and decision-ready — title, litigation, builder, price and cost in one score — and tells you whether the purchase is worth paying a lawyer to close.

What if the title has a problem?

You get the finding, the source, and a walk-away or negotiate recommendation before your money is committed. Catching it here is the whole point.

Can you check a resale flat, not just a new project?

Yes. Resale needs the society share certificate and the chain of conveyance as well, which we include.
