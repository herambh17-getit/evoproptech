# BuySafe by Evoproptech — brand sheet

Internal reference. Not published: `brand/` is excluded from the live site by `.vercelignore`.

## 1. Naming system

| Level | Name | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Brand | **BuySafe by Evoproptech** | First mention, page titles, structured data, social bios, email footers |
| Short form | **BuySafe** | Everything after the first mention: buttons, FAQ, WhatsApp, conversation |
| Product | **BuySafe Report** | The paid deliverable (₹8,499) |
| Output | **Property Health Score** | The 0–100 result inside the report, plus GO / CAUTION / STOP |
| Legal entity | **EVO Proptech Advisory** | Contracts, invoices, privacy policy, terms, footer ("operating as BuySafe by Evoproptech") |

Spelling rules
- **BuySafe**: one word, capital B, capital S. Never *Buysafe, Buy-Safe, Buy Safe, EVO BuySafe*.
- **by Evoproptech**: lowercase "by", then a single capital E. Never *by EVO* or *by EVOPROPTECH* in running text.
- Product name is title case: **BuySafe Report**, not "BuySafe report".
- Why the output keeps its descriptive name: "Property Health Score" is what buyers search for. The brand owns the report, not the metric.
- The domain (`evoproptech.com`) and email (`hello@evoproptech.com`) do not change.

## 2. Positioning

- **One line:** Independent property due diligence for Indian homebuyers — one score, one clear call, before you commit.
- **Tagline:** Know before you commit.
- **Three proofs, each backed by something on the site:**
  1. *Transparent pricing* — you pay ₹8,499 for the report; if you buy through us the developer pays a referral fee, disclosed upfront and never changing your price (written into the terms).
  2. *Sourced* — every finding names the record it came from.
  3. *Clear* — one score and a GO / CAUTION / STOP, not a folder of documents.
- **What we are not** (stated in the terms): a broker, a law firm, a valuer, or a SEBI-registered adviser.

## 3. Voice

Plain, calm, specific. Evidence before adjectives. Say what we cannot do.

- Do: "Your fee is the same whether the report says buy or walk away."
- Don't: "Don't miss out — limited slots!" (false urgency was removed from the site on purpose)
- Numbers only when we can source them. Official rates (stamp duty, GST) beat invented ranges.

## 4. Visual identity

- **Mark:** copper tile with five cut-out bands (`assets/logo.svg`). Do not recolour, stretch or add effects. The cut-outs show whatever surface the mark sits on, as in the original artwork.
- **Lockup:** mark, then **BuySafe** (Playfair Display 700, 22px in the header) over *by Evoproptech* (Inter 500, 12px). Never show the wordmark without the mark.
- **Colour:** copper `#D4521A` for large display use only. Small brand-coloured text uses `#B04010` (5.7:1). Buttons fill `#BC4614` (white text 5.2:1). Ink `#14100D`, cream `#FFFCFA`. White on `#D4521A` is 4.18:1 and fails AA, so never use it for text.
- **Type:** Playfair Display 700 for headlines and the wordmark; Inter for everything else. Both self-hosted.
- **Files:** `assets/logo.svg` (master), `logo-512.png`, `favicon.*`, `og-image.png` (rebuild from `assets/og-image.html`), `brand/gen-logo.js` (regenerates the mark).

## 6. Open items (not legal advice)

- **Trademark.** Search "BuySafe" before printing or heavy ad spend (Indian Trade Marks Registry; consider a trademark attorney). Two risks: a name made of two plain words (buy + safe) is harder to register and to defend, and similar names may already be registered. I recall an unrelated US company using "buySAFE" for online-seller bonding, which I could not verify. Treat this as a prompt to check, not a finding.
- **Off-site profiles to rename:** Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business display name, Meta page and ad account, LinkedIn, Privyr account name, email display name, invoice template.
- **Google's site name** updates from the structured data on its own schedule, usually weeks.
