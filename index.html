    Where the money goes

    Six ways buyers lose lakhs — and only find out afterwards

    None of these are exotic edge cases. Every one of them is visible in public records before you sign, and almost nobody looks.

    Legal

    Title left unverified

    Skipping the encumbrance and title chain is how disputes surface years after possession. A clean title means tracing 30+ years of history, not glancing at one deed.

    Financial

    Paying above market without knowing it

    Builder price is an asking price. Without recent comparable transactions on the same micro-market and configuration, you have no way to know what the flat is actually worth.

    Compliance

    RERA flags nobody opened

    Registration status, revised completion dates and complaints filed against the project sit on the MahaRERA portal in public view. Most buyers never open it.

    Research

    Builder history taken on trust

    Delivery slippage on past projects, litigation, and financial stress are all traceable. A show flat and a glossy brochure tell you nothing about any of it.

    Financial

    The cost stack nobody quotes

    In Maharashtra, stamp duty is 6% in Mumbai (5% plus 1% metro cess), registration 1% capped at ₹30,000, and GST 5% on an under-construction flat with no input credit. Club fees, parking and maintenance deposits sit on top — each disclosed one line at a time.

    Location

    Appreciation assumed, not checked

    Committed infrastructure moves prices; announced infrastructure often does not. Which one your location is actually getting is a matter of record.

    Questions

    What buyers ask us first

    Is EVO a broker or a real estate agent?

    No. We never list, market or sell property, and we take no commission from any builder, broker or developer. You pay us a flat fee and we work only for you — which is precisely why our report is free to tell you not to buy.

    How is this different from hiring a lawyer?

    A lawyer verifies the legal title and reviews your agreement. That work matters, and we recommend engaging one before registration.

    Our scope is wider. Alongside title and encumbrance work, we benchmark the asking price against actual registered transactions, audit the builder's delivery record and RERA complaint history, and calculate your true all-in cost. The output is one decision-ready score rather than a bundle of documents you still have to interpret.

    What if I haven't shortlisted anything yet?

    That is often the best time to talk to us. We will help you set a realistic budget including hidden costs, map the micro-markets that actually match your requirement, and define what to rule out — so your site visits are spent on properties worth visiting.

    How long does the report take?

    48 to 72 hours from the point we have your property details. Briefs submitted before 5pm are queued the same day. If a particular public record is slow to return, we tell you upfront rather than holding the whole report back.

    Is ₹8,499 worth it on a ₹1–5 crore purchase?

    It is about 0.04% of a ₹2 crore purchase. The risks we screen for — a disputed title, a price never benchmarked, an undisclosed cost stack — are each measured in lakhs. You are buying certainty on the largest cheque you are likely to write.

    Do you cover properties outside Mumbai?

    Mumbai and the MMR are our core coverage, where our comparable-transaction data is deepest. We take selected assignments in Pune, Bengaluru and the NCR — send us the property and we will confirm coverage before you pay anything.

    What happens if the score comes back low?

    You get the report, the reasoning and a call — and you have saved yourself from the purchase. A STOP recommendation is the most valuable output we produce, and it costs the same as a GO.
