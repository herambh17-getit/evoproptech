South Mumbai (SoBo) — Colaba, Fort, Marine Lines, Malabar Hill and around — is the oldest part of the city. Much of its stock is older buildings, a good share of them cessed and tenanted (pagdi), and many sit inside heritage precincts. Here the questions go beyond a clean title to what interest in the property you actually get, and what the building can and cannot do.

What to pay extra attention to in South Mumbai

In South Mumbai the distinctive things to confirm are whether a building is cessed (which brings MHADA repair rules and a specific redevelopment path), ownership versus tenanted (pagdi) status, and any heritage-precinct restrictions that limit alterations or redevelopment — alongside the title chain and society conveyance. Because cessed and cluster redevelopment has its own approvals, the redevelopment position is worth confirming before you commit.

  • Cessed-building & redevelopment status. Whether the building is cessed (MHADA), its repair / redevelopment position, and any cluster-redevelopment scheme.
  • Ownership vs tenancy (pagdi). Whether the flat is full ownership or tenanted, and exactly what rights transfer to you.
  • Title chain & encumbrance. Ownership traced back and the registered charges pulled, so no dispute surfaces after possession.
  • MahaRERA status. Registration, revised completion dates and complaints for the specific project and phase.
  • Builder track record. Delivery history, litigation and financial-stress signals for the developer.
  • Price benchmarking. The asking price against registered comparable sales in the same micro-market.
  • Total cost of ownership. Stamp duty, registration, GST and every builder-side charge, totalled upfront.

South Mumbai questions

What is a cessed building, and why does it matter in South Mumbai?

A cessed building is an older island-city building (generally built before 1969) that pays a MHADA repair cess and falls under specific repair and redevelopment rules. It affects what can be done with the property and how a redevelopment works, so we confirm the cess position before you commit.

Is a pagdi (tenanted) flat in South Mumbai the same as owning it?

No. In a pagdi arrangement you hold tenancy rights rather than outright ownership, with different transfer and resale conditions. We confirm whether a South Mumbai flat is ownership or tenanted so you know exactly what you are buying.
