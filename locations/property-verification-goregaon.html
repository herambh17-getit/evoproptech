Goregaon, on the western line, has seen a steady run of large-format and under-construction projects alongside its older housing stock. When a flat is still being built, the risk shifts from old-title questions towards whether the project gets delivered on time and as promised.

What to pay extra attention to in Goregaon

In Goregaon the emphasis often falls on builder delivery and MahaRERA: the developer’s record of finishing earlier projects, the declared completion date against every revision, and the sanctioned plan against the brochure. Buying under construction also means the full cost stack — GST, stamp duty, registration and builder-side charges — is larger and worth totalling before you pay a token.

  • Delivery-date history. Original versus revised MahaRERA completion dates for the project and the builder’s other projects.
  • Title chain & encumbrance. Ownership traced back and the registered charges pulled, so no dispute surfaces after possession.
  • MahaRERA status. Registration, revised completion dates and complaints for the specific project and phase.
  • Builder track record. Delivery history, litigation and financial-stress signals for the developer.
  • Price benchmarking. The asking price against registered comparable sales in the same micro-market.
  • Total cost of ownership. Stamp duty, registration, GST and every builder-side charge, totalled upfront.

Goregaon questions

What should I check before booking an under-construction flat in Goregaon?

The project’s MahaRERA registration and completion-date history, the builder’s delivery record on earlier projects, the sanctioned plan against the brochure, and the full cost including GST. We cover all of these in one report.

Does GST apply to a Goregaon under-construction flat?

Yes — 5% with no input tax credit on a standard under-construction flat, and nil once the occupancy certificate is issued. We total the all-in cost so the final number holds no surprises.
