Navi Mumbai was planned and developed by CIDCO, and a lot of its land is held on long lease from CIDCO rather than as freehold. That single fact changes what diligence looks like here: tenure, transfer permissions and any dues to the authority can matter as much as the title chain itself.

What to pay extra attention to in Navi Mumbai

In Navi Mumbai the distinctive thing to confirm is land tenure and transfer — whether the plot is CIDCO-leased, what the lease terms and any transfer or NOC requirements are, and whether dues are clear — alongside the usual title, RERA and builder checks. With new infrastructure (the airport, metro and road links) driving the market, we also benchmark price against what comparable flats have actually registered at rather than projected appreciation.

  • Land tenure & transfer. Whether the plot is CIDCO-leased, the lease terms, and any transfer / NOC requirements or dues.
  • Title chain & encumbrance. Ownership traced back and the registered charges pulled, so no dispute surfaces after possession.
  • MahaRERA status. Registration, revised completion dates and complaints for the specific project and phase.
  • Builder track record. Delivery history, litigation and financial-stress signals for the developer.
  • Price benchmarking. The asking price against registered comparable sales in the same micro-market.
  • Total cost of ownership. Stamp duty, registration, GST and every builder-side charge, totalled upfront.

Navi Mumbai questions

What is special about buying property in Navi Mumbai?

Much of Navi Mumbai sits on land leased from CIDCO rather than freehold, so confirming the tenure, the transfer or NOC requirements and any outstanding dues matters here more than in the island city. We check this alongside the title and RERA.

Is CIDCO-leased property safe to buy?

It is bought and sold routinely, but you should know the lease terms and transfer conditions before you commit, not after. We confirm the tenure and the steps involved as part of the report.
