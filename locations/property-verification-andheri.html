Andheri is one of Mumbai’s largest and most mixed suburbs — old societies, fresh redevelopment and new towers sit side by side across West and East, around the metro and the Western and Eastern Express Highways. That mix is exactly why a single checklist does not fit every Andheri flat: an old society resale and a new launch carry very different risks.

What to pay extra attention to in Andheri

In Andheri the two things that most often need a closer look are redevelopment and conveyance on older societies — whether the land has actually been conveyed to the society, and what any redevelopment agreement commits you to — and, on new launches, a fresh MahaRERA read. Because prices vary sharply from one lane to the next, benchmarking against registered sales in the same pocket matters more here than a suburb-wide average.

  • Conveyance & redevelopment. For older societies: whether land is conveyed, and the terms of any redevelopment agreement.
  • Title chain & encumbrance. Ownership traced back and the registered charges pulled, so no dispute surfaces after possession.
  • MahaRERA status. Registration, revised completion dates and complaints for the specific project and phase.
  • Builder track record. Delivery history, litigation and financial-stress signals for the developer.
  • Price benchmarking. The asking price against registered comparable sales in the same micro-market.
  • Total cost of ownership. Stamp duty, registration, GST and every builder-side charge, totalled upfront.

Andheri questions

Is buying a resale flat in an old Andheri society risky?

It can be, if conveyance of the society’s land was never completed or a redevelopment is pending — both affect your rights and resale. We confirm the conveyance status, the society share certificate and the chain of ownership before you commit.

Do you check new launches in Andheri too?

Yes. For a new or under-construction project we read the full MahaRERA entry — registration, revised completion dates, sanctioned plan and complaints — alongside the builder’s delivery record.
