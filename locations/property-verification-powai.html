Powai is one of Mumbai’s more established, premium pockets, built out around large complexes and the lake. Because most transactions here are resale rather than new launches, the questions shift towards the society and the specific flat’s history rather than a project still under construction.

What to pay extra attention to in Powai

In Powai the focus is on resale diligence: the society share certificate and the chain of conveyance, whether the building’s land is conveyed, and any outstanding dues or transfer conditions on the flat. At the premium end, price benchmarking against actual registered sales in the same complex matters — headline asking prices and real transacted prices can diverge.

  • Resale & society checks. Society share certificate, chain of conveyance, conveyance status and any outstanding dues on the flat.
  • Title chain & encumbrance. Ownership traced back and the registered charges pulled, so no dispute surfaces after possession.
  • MahaRERA status. Registration, revised completion dates and complaints for the specific project and phase.
  • Builder track record. Delivery history, litigation and financial-stress signals for the developer.
  • Price benchmarking. The asking price against registered comparable sales in the same micro-market.
  • Total cost of ownership. Stamp duty, registration, GST and every builder-side charge, totalled upfront.
